# STYLING & UI LIBRARIES REFERENCE

### 1. expo-linear-gradient
- Dashboards/Heroes: `['#10b981', '#059669']` (Emerald) or `['#042F2E', '#0D9488']` (Teal) in `(donor)/index.tsx`, `(shelter)/index.tsx`, `(consumer)/profile.tsx`.
- Urgency (<1h left): `['#f59e0b', '#ef4444']` in `(views)/surplus/[id].tsx`, `(consumer)/index.tsx`.
- Primary CTAs: `['#042F2E', '#0D9488']` or `['#3b82f6', '#2563eb']`.
- Pattern: `<LinearGradient colors={['#042F2E', '#0D9488']} start={{x:0, y:0}} end={{x:1, y:1}} style={styles.card} />`

### 2. expo-blur
- Overlays & floating cards: `intensity={50-60}`, `tint="light"|"dark"` in `(consumer)/map.tsx`, `(shelter)/map.tsx`, modal sheets.
- Pattern: `<BlurView intensity={60} tint="dark" style={styles.glassCard}>{children}</BlurView>`

### 3. expo-haptics
- QR Scan / Claim Success: `Haptics.notificationAsync(Haptics.NotificationFeedbackType.Success)`
- Action / Button tap: `Haptics.impactAsync(Haptics.ImpactFeedbackStyle.Medium)`
- Stepper / Select: `Haptics.selectionAsync()`

### 4. @shopify/react-native-skia
- Neon glowing drop-shadows & circular impact progress rings in `(donor)/tax.tsx` and `(donor)/profile.tsx`.

### 5. lottie-react-native
- Empty states & celebratory illustrations in feeds & verified screens.
- Pattern: `<LottieView source={require('../../assets/lottie/empty-box.json')} autoPlay loop style={{width: 180, height: 180}} />`

### 6. moti & moti/skeleton
- Skeletons: `<Skeleton colorMode="dark" width="100%" height={120} radius={16} />` (never use raw spinners).
- Staggered entrances: `<MotiView from={{opacity: 0, translateY: 15}} animate={{opacity: 1, translateY: 0}} transition={{type: 'timing', duration: 350, delay: index * 60}} />`
- Pulsing badges: `animate={{scale: [1, 1.05, 1]}} transition={{loop: true, duration: 2000}}`

### 7. react-native-confetti-cannon
- Pickup verified & tax milestones in `(views)/receipt/[id].tsx`, `(views)/success/[id].tsx`, `(donor)/tax.tsx`.
- Pattern: `<ConfettiCannon count={80} origin={{x: -10, y: 0}} fadeOut autoStart />`

### 8. @gorhom/bottom-sheet
- Map pin surplus preview & filter sheets in `(consumer)/map.tsx`, `(shelter)/map.tsx`.
- Pattern: `<BottomSheet snapPoints={['25%', '50%', '85%']} index={0}><BottomSheetView>{content}</BottomSheetView></BottomSheet>`

### 9. react-native-reanimated-carousel
- Multi-photo food galleries on `(views)/surplus/[id].tsx`.
- Pattern: `<Carousel width={screenWidth - 32} height={200} data={images} renderItem={({item}) => <Image source={{uri: item}} style={styles.img} />} />`

### 10. react-native-toast-message
- Status alerts replacing native `Alert.alert` across all screens.
- Pattern: `Toast.show({ type: 'success'|'error', text1: 'Title', text2: 'Subtitle', position: 'top', visibilityTime: 3000 });`

### 11. System & Device Engagement
- expo-notifications: Push & local alerts for bulk donations and urgent expirations (<30m).
- expo-print: Generate official 80G tax receipt PDF directly in `(donor)/tax.tsx`.
- expo-sharing: Native OS share sheet for surplus listing dissemination.
- expo-local-authentication: Biometric login for verified kitchens and shelters.
